## Chapter 1: Going Grey

The most colourful places on Earth are turning grey overnight. Somebody is stealing colour.

> **Pip:** So I built you a partner who is made of colour. Rory, meet PALETTE.
> **PALETTE:** Hello! I'm a chameleon. A robot one. I can be any colour I like, and today I'd like to be all of them.
> **Rory:** Can you really turn invisible?

> **Pip:** Your watch fires paint now. Training first. Follow the glowing beacons!
