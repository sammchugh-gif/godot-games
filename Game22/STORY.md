# Agent Rory: Spectrum — the story

Somebody is stealing colour. Not gold, not gravity, not the sea: colour. The
painted shopfronts of Dingle went grey overnight, every one. The blue tiles of
Lisbon are white and grey. The pink is gone out of the flamingos. And the
person taking it, Baroness Grisaille, thinks she is doing the world a favour.

## The idea

Every place in the game starts drained. The ground, the houses, the sky, the
water and the people in it are grey, all but a small bubble of colour round
Rory himself, because Rory and PALETTE are made of it. Every mission you win
paints a circle of the world back in, with a rainbow ripple running out from
the beacon; when the last mission in a place is done, the whole place floods
with colour and the band plays its full tune. That is the game: twelve grey
places, and you leave every one of them bright.

## The cast

- **Agent Rory**, POLARIS's youngest agent. His watch fires paint now.
- **PALETTE**, a chameleon robot Pip built to be Rory's partner. She can be any
  colour she likes, and she has an opinion about all of them. In a sneak she
  fades to nearly nothing. She talks the way a chameleon would if it could:
  slowly, sideways, and always about what colour she is at the moment.
- **Pip**, POLARIS's gadget-maker, on the earpiece.
- **Admiral Frost**, who runs POLARIS and takes his tea with milk and no sugar.
- **Baroness Grisaille**, the Collector of Colour. Grey coat, grey hat, grey
  gloves. She finds colour vulgar and loud, and she is collecting all of it
  into jars for a gallery in the sky where nobody will ever have to look at it.
- **The Blotters**, her robots: round grey blobs with a mop stuck in the top,
  two worried eyes and a drip. They mop colour up. Splat one with paint and it
  swells and pops into confetti.
- **The Big Blot** and **the Eraser**, the two giants.
- **The friends**: Saoirse in Dingle, Tiago in Lisbon, Camila in Guatapé, Wara
  on the salt, Aino in Lapland, Layla at Petra, Linh in Hội An, Fara among the
  baobabs, Dilnoza in Samarkand, Marta at the lakes and Tuli in the dunes.

## Chapter one: Going Grey

**Dingle, Ireland.** Frost sends Rory to a harbour whose painted street has
lost its paint. Pip introduces PALETTE, and the harbour is the training ground:
colour drops to collect, practice Blotters to splat, the last red door on the
street to find, a rainbow run through six gates in order, and the mixing desk
where red and yellow make orange.

**Lisbon, Portugal.** The trams have gone grey and the azulejo tiles are white.
The drops rolled down the steps of Alfama; the number 28 tram carries some on
its roof and Rory rides it to get them. The Blotters carry a card: BARONESS
GRISAILLE, COLLECTOR OF COLOUR. The old elevator's colour lock opens the way up
to the castle, where the Baroness introduces herself, and her Blotters flee
down the hill in a tram with Rory in the other one.

**Guatapé, Colombia.** The most painted town in the world: every house wears
zócalos, pictures along its base, and Camila's grandpa painted half of them.
Four of his paintings still have their colour. The drops climbed the seven
hundred steps of El Peñol; the Blotters' boat is on the lake; the boat yard is
full of them.

**Salar de Uyuni, Bolivia.** The biggest mirror in the world, with a skin of
water on the salt and the sky under your feet. Grey flamingos are just big
pigeons. A jeep across the flat, salt blocks to stack by the salt hotel, the
old trains pulled up off their rails, and a machine that wants flamingo pink.

**Lapland, Finland.** The northern lights have gone grey. Red cabins, a frozen
lake for the husky sleds, the ice hotel where the machine keeps the aurora in
a jar, and reindeer to rescue. The sky comes back green and pink and purple.

## Chapter two: The Collector

**Petra, Jordan.** A city carved into rose-red cliffs that didn't glow this
morning. Camels down the Siq, the long climb to the High Place, a sneak
through the Treasury for the key to her jars, and the Big Blot.

**Hội An, Vietnam.** Lantern festival night with every lantern dark. Four are
still lit. Blotters in a sampan on the river, a flight in a sky lantern
through rings over the roofs, drops on the Japanese Bridge, and Blotters in
the market mopping the dragon fruit.

**The Avenue of the Baobabs, Madagascar.** Sunset, trees a thousand years old,
and chameleons everywhere, all grey. PALETTE takes it personally. Five cousins
still have a little colour; the drops are on the treetops; a Blotter has
stolen a zebu cart; the lemurs are being lifted off the branches.

**Samarkand, Uzbekistan.** The Registan, four hundred years of blue, and a
bazaar of grey saffron. Drops on the domes and minarets, five sacks of spice
that still have their colour, the Baroness's workshop behind the silks with
her plans for something called the Monochrome, and four hundred blues to mix.

**Plitvice Lakes, Croatia.** Sixteen lakes one above the other, turquoise and
green, now a black-and-white film. Rory swims for drops on the lake bed, climbs
the boardwalks past the falls, finds her jars in the murk by colour pulse, and
opens the sluice she shut.

## Chapter three: Full Spectrum

**Sossusvlei, Namibia.** The reddest dunes in the world and Deadvlei, a white
pan with black trees six hundred years old. The Monochrome, her airship, hangs
over it all, drinking the red. A buggy over the dunes, the black trees to find
(even black is a colour), Big Daddy's ridge, and the gangway of grey beams up
to the ship.

**The Monochrome.** A flying gallery where every colour in the world hangs in
a jar. A sneak through the gallery of grey pictures, the colour vault, the
engine that runs on grey and stalls on colour, a rainbow run round the deck,
and the Eraser, with the Baroness driving. She ends up pink. Frost offers her
the POLARIS art room, on one condition: the children get to paint over her
grey. She finds, to her surprise, that she rather likes the idea.
