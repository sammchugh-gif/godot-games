## The prologue: POLARIS HQ, today

*The briefing:*

> **Admiral Frost:** Agent Rory. Thank you for coming so quickly. History is leaking.
> **Rory:** Leaking?
> **Admiral Frost:** Great moments are going missing. A cave painting. The top stone of the Great Pyramid. The Olympic flame. One after another.
> **Pip:** And every time one goes, the present goes a bit wobbly. Look at my tea! It turned back into tea leaves. In the cup!
> **Pip:** And the clock's been going backwards all day.
> **Admiral Frost:** The one taking them is Doctor Hourglass. She's bottling history's great moments to power a machine that skips the whole world straight into the future.
> **Rory:** Why would anyone want to skip everything?
> **Admiral Frost:** She can't bear to wait for anything. Not even the kettle.
> **Dr Flint:** Which is where I come in. I'm Dr Flint, and this is my time-sled.
> **Dr Flint:** The leak starts a very long way back: sixty-six million years. Collect your Chrono-watch from my bench, and we'll follow it.

*The Chrono-watch:*

> **Dr Flint:** That's the Chrono-watch. At the moment it only tells the time.
> **Rory:** Only?
> **Dr Flint:** It learns. Every few eras it'll pick up a new trick. You'll see.
> **Dr Flint:** Now, the time-sled. The front seat's yours.

*The launch:*

> **Dr Flint:** Strapped in? Good. Pip, open the roof!
> **Pip:** Opening the roof!
> **Admiral Frost:** Bring history back, Agent Rory. All of it.
> **Rory:** See you soon. Or a very long time ago.
> **Pip:** Time-sled launching in three... two... one!

## Act 1: Deep Time

Somebody is stealing history. Follow them back to the dinosaurs.

### Dinosaur Valley, 66 million years ago

> **Dr Flint:** Sixty-six million years ago. Welcome to the age of the dinosaurs.
> **Rory:** It's so warm. And everything is enormous!
> **Admiral Frost:** We can hear you, Rory. The leak is strongest right where you've landed.
> **Dr Flint:** Those glowing sparks are bits of broken time. Grab them and we can follow the trail.

**Mission 1: Strange Footprints** (Puzzle, level 1: whose footprints?)

> **Dr Flint:** Look at the mud. Footprints! All sorts of creatures have walked through here.
> **Pip:** Whose tracks are they, Rory? Look at the toes, then tap who made them.

*After:*

> **Rory:** Little round ones with sand in them. Not a dinosaur's. And they go all the way to the nests.
> **Dr Flint:** Something's stealing from the dinosaurs. Come on, quietly.

*Intel: Sandy footprints.* Small round footprints with sand in them lead across the valley to the dinosaur nests.

**Mission 2: Egg Thieves** (Puzzle, level 1: wrong time!)

> **Pip:** Robots! Shaped like egg timers. They're stealing dinosaur eggs!
> **Dr Flint:** And dropping things from our time all over the valley. Tap whatever doesn't belong here, Rory, and we'll find the robot.

*After:*

> **Rory:** Got one! Upside down you go.
> **Sandbot:** Upside down! Now I have to wait for my sand. I hate waiting!
> **Rory:** Who sent you?
> **Sandbot:** Doctor Hourglass. She'll be very cross. She's cross about most things.

*Intel: Doctor Hourglass.* The egg-timer robots are Sandbots. They work for Doctor Hourglass, who hates waiting.

**Mission 3: Back to the Nest** (Puzzle, level 1: fossil puzzle)

> **Dr Flint:** The Sandbots dug up the nests, looking for eggs. And they've jumbled up an old fossil underneath.
> **Dr Flint:** Even in the age of the dinosaurs, there are fossils. Of much older dinosaurs!
> **Pip:** Tap the stones to turn them until the skeleton is whole, Rory. Then the eggs can go back on top.

*After:*

> **Rory:** The fossil's fixed, and the eggs are back in the nest, all safe and warm. Wait. That one's wobbling.
> **Dr Flint:** Rory. It's hatching. Right now. In your hands.
> **Pebble:** Honk?
> **Rory:** Hello. I'm Rory. I'm not your mum.
> **Pip:** She's decided. You're hers now. What shall we call her?
> **Rory:** Pebble. She looks like a pebble with legs.

*Intel: Pebble.* A baby triceratops hatched in Rory's hands. She's called Pebble, and she thinks Rory is her mum.

**Mission 4: Tiptoe Past the T. rex** (Puzzle, level 1: river crossing)

> **Dr Flint:** The Sandbots' cart went over the river, right past a sleeping T. rex.
> **Pip:** The raft holds Rory and one more. Get everyone across, quietly, before it wakes!
> **Pebble:** Mrrp.

*After:*

> **Rory:** Past it, with time to spare. Pebble didn't make a sound.
> **Dr Flint:** Clever girl. And there's the cart, full of eggs.

*Intel: The egg cart.* The Sandbots are getting away down the valley with a cart full of eggs.

**Mission 5: The Egg Cart** (Chase, level 1)

> **Dr Flint:** They're getting away with the eggs! Take the time-sled. It hovers.
> **Pip:** Drag to steer. Catch the cart and TAG it!

*After:*

> **Rory:** Tagged! The eggs are safe. But the Sandbots jumped into a glowing hole in the air.
> **Dr Flint:** A time tunnel. We follow it.

*Intel: A time tunnel.* The Sandbots escaped through a time tunnel. It leads to the Ice Age.

*Leaving:*

> **Dr Flint:** The Sandbots' cart went back into the time tunnel. The trail leads to the Ice Age.
> **Rory:** Pebble, you can't come. It's cold there. And you're a dinosaur.
> **Pebble:** Honk!
> **Dr Flint:** She's already on the sled. I don't think she's asking.

### The Ice Age, 20,000 years ago

**Mission 8: Snow Wall** (Puzzle, level 1: shape fit)

*After:*

**Mission 9: Mammoth Ride** (Mammoth ride, level 1)

*After:*

> **Tuva:** The Sandbots dropped the painting! It's rolled up, like a rug.

*Intel: The painting.* The Sandbots dropped the painting. It only stays on the wall if the handprints are touched in order.

**Mission 10: Handprints** (Code lock, level 1)

*After:*

> **Admiral Frost:** Next stop, the pyramids. Four and a half thousand years ago.
