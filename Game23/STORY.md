# Agent Rory: Timeslip — the story bible

FILE 006, Game23. A time-travel caper on the Deep Red engine: twelve eras, from the
dinosaurs to a city in the year 2150, and a baby dinosaur who comes along. Sixty
missions in two acts of thirty (five an era: puzzle missions and one action), released an act at a time, each act complete and
tested before it goes live.

## The story in one breath

History is leaking. The last stone of the Great Pyramid never arrives, a Viking sail
goes missing, the first aeroplane never leaves the sand, and every time a great moment
vanishes the present flickers: Pip's tea turns back into leaves and the HQ clock runs
backwards. POLARIS traces it to **Doctor Hourglass**, an inventor who cannot bear to
wait for anything. She is bottling history's great moments to power her
**Fast-Forward Engine**, which will skip the whole world straight to the shiny future
she has designed, where nobody ever waits, practises or grows up slowly again. Rory
takes the POLARIS **time-sled** after her.

It starts in the present, in the **Time Room at POLARIS HQ**. Frost briefs Rory in front of
the history wall, where the great moments flicker and go out one by one. Pip's tea has turned
back into leaves and the clock on the wall runs backwards. Dr Flint gives Rory the
Chrono-watch, which only tells the time so far. Rory and Dr Flint climb onto the time-sled,
Pip opens the roof, and the sled goes up through it into the time tunnel.

On the first jump, into Dinosaur Valley, Rory saves a nest of eggs from Hourglass's
Sandbots. The last egg hatches in his hands. The baby triceratops decides he is her
mum, and when the time-sled leaves she jumps in after him. She is **Pebble**, and
she is his partner for the rest of the game. She grows a little in every era, and by
the second act she is big enough to ride.

The theme is the one Hourglass has wrong: good things take time. Pebble can't be
rushed, and she is the best thing that happens to Rory in the whole game.

## Cast

- **Rory**: our agent. New kit: the Chrono-watch, which learns a new power in each of
  the first eras (slow-time, then blink-back, then echo). In each era he wears a
  disguise over his suit so he fits in: a fur cloak, linen, a tunic, a tricorn hat.
- **Pebble**: a baby triceratops built from simple shapes, in the same way as
  Spectrum's PALETTE. She has three nubby horns, a frill that flushes pink when she's
  happy, big eyes and a stubby tail. She follows Rory and sniffs out hidden things,
  pointing her nose and snuffling. She charges crates and Sandbots, and she honks,
  squeaks and purrs rather than talking. She grows each era, and from Act Two Rory
  can ride her, which is how the new chases work.
- **Dr Juniper Flint**: POLARIS's time scientist, who built the time-sled. She comes
  on the first jump and runs the Time Room after that.
- **Admiral Frost** and **Pip**: POLARIS, in the Time Room at HQ.
- **Doctor Hourglass**: the villain. Fast-talking and brilliant, and she hates queues,
  kettles and birthdays that take a year to come round. She wears a long coat of glass
  buttons that trickle sand. Signature line: "Why wait?"
- **The Sandbots**: her little hourglass-shaped robots, which run on sand. When one is
  bubbled it flips upside down and has to wait for its sand to run through before it
  can move again, which it hates more than anything.
- **Wren**: Hourglass's apprentice, an inventor about Rory's age who built the
  Sandbots. Wren turns ally in Act Two on finding out that the Fast-Forward Engine
  would skip everyone's childhood too.
- **Contacts**: one made-up local per era. No real historical person is a character;
  famous people are only mentioned (the brothers at Kitty Hawk are figures in the
  distance and are never named).

(Meltdown's Operation Midnight already has a clock-stopping villain with henchmen
called Tick and Tock. Hourglass is about sand and skipping ahead rather than
clockwork, and her robots are Sandbots, so the two don't blur together.)

## New to play

1. **Slow-time** (from era 2): hold the watch button and the world slows to a crawl
   while Rory keeps full speed. It is a clock scale on everything but Rory. It makes
   lasers, falling things and chases fair for a young player, and it recharges.
2. **Blink-back** (from era 3): Rory jumps back to where he was three seconds ago,
   like an undo after a missed jump. It moves only Rory, so the physics stays sound.
3. **Echo missions** (from era 4): press ECHO and walk somewhere, then press it again. Rory
   jumps back to where he started, and a see-through blue copy of him walks the same way and
   stays where it ends up: on the plate that holds a door open, say, while Rory goes through.
   The echo is a recorded path replayed on a second body that presses plates.
4. **Pebble** as a partner: she sniffs out the hidden (a mission kind, and a hint
   button), charges Sandbots over in round-ups, and from Act Two can be ridden.
5. **Four new mission kinds**:
   - **Dig**: brush and chip a grid of sand and rock to uncover a buried find (a
     fossil, a carved stone, a lost key) without cracking it.
   - **Timeline**: put picture cards of events in the right order before the sand
     runs out.
   - **Ride**: Pebble (or a mammoth, a camel or a horse) runs a track; steer, jump
     and duck to stay on and collect sparks.
   - **Echo**: plates, doors and levers that need two of you, as above.

With Deep Red's 29 kinds (cells, round-up, stack, tractor, drone, lasers, chase,
stealth, boss, circuit, codes, drive, divers, tide, morse, dive, sub rings, boat
chase, sub chase, sonar, salvage, escort, surf, valves, airlock, current, star map,
greenhouse, climb), that makes 33. Not all of them suit history; those that do are
re-skinned for each era.

The collectibles are **time sparks**: bits of torn time that fizz in the air where a
moment was stolen.

End of Act One: the Serpent collapses into a beach, the sail goes back on the
longship, and Hourglass leaps forward through time. Something drops as she goes: a
blueprint signed by Wren.

### Act Two: Clockwork (from castles to the future)

| # | Era | Contact | What Hourglass is taking |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | A castle town, the first printing workshop | Hal, a squire | the first printed page |
| 8 | The Great Wall and the treasure fleet | Mei, a lantern-maker's daughter | the fleet's great ship on the day it sails |
| 9 | The age of sail, a survey ship charting a new coast | Jack, the ship's boy | the first map of the coast |
| 10 | The steam railway's opening day | Maisie, the station-master's daughter | the first passenger train |
| 11 | Kitty Hawk, 1903 | Sam, from the life-saving station | the first powered flight |
| 12 | The year 2150, Hourglass's city | Wren and Dr Flint | everything: the Fast-Forward Engine starts |

Pebble is big enough to ride from era 7, so Act Two has Pebble rides through the
castle market, along the Great Wall and over the Kitty Hawk dunes. Wren turns in
era 9, aboard the survey ship. The detailed mission list for Act Two is written
before it is built, as Deep Red's acts were.

The game ends with the Engine running backwards and every stolen moment flying home:
the capstone settles, the sail fills and the aeroplane lifts off the sand. Hourglass
has to wait in a queue for the first time in her life, and finds it isn't so bad.
Rory takes Pebble back to Dinosaur Valley, where her herd is, but her mother nudges
her back towards Rory. Pebble comes home to POLARIS, and from then on she lives in
the Time Room and the HQ. Dr Flint: "One little triceratops won't change history.
Probably."

## How it's built

- **Engine**: a fork of Deep Red (Game21) into Game23, keeping its tooling
  (`tools/missions.mjs` autopilot for every mission from its beacon, `beacons.mjs`,
  `levelcheck.mjs`, `perf.mjs`, the lane runner) and its budgets (at most 317 draw
  calls and 331k triangles in a place).
- **Pebble** is a partner module with the same small API as BOLT and PALETTE (`root`,
  `pos`, `play(name)`, `update(dt)`, `walkTo`), plus `grow(era)` and a rideable mode
  for Act Two.
- **The watch powers** are engine-wide: slow-time is a time scale on the world's
  update that leaves the player alone; blink-back is a three-second ring buffer of
  Rory's position; echo is a recorded input track replayed on a second body.
- **Each era is a level file** with its own sky, ground and kit (ferns, glaciers,
  pyramids, temples, arches and fjords for Act One), built and tested one era at a
  time.
- **Voices**: every line recorded and checked by the speech recogniser, with a new
  cast file. Pebble's honks and purrs are sound effects, not speech.
- **HQ**: FILE 006 on the shelf and the mission board, as the other games are.
