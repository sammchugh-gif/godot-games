## Act 1: The Sea Is Going Down

Harbours are draining and the tide has stopped. Somebody is stealing the sea.

### The Narwhal, Scotland

> **Admiral Frost:** Agent Rory. The sea is going down.
> **Admiral Frost:** Harbours are draining. Boats are sitting on mud. And in Canada the tide has simply stopped coming in.
> **Pip:** So we're taking the Narwhal to sea. And I've built you a partner for the water. Rory, meet TORPEDO.
> **TORPEDO:** Hello! I'm a submarine. A small one. The best one.
> **BOLT:** I am also here. I am waterproof now. Mostly.
> **TORPEDO:** Mostly?
> **BOLT:** Mostly.
> **Pip:** Training first. Follow the glowing beacons!

**Mission 1: Crystal School** (Tide Crystals, level 1)

> **Pip:** These blue crystals are Tide Crystals. Undertow's machines freeze seawater into them.
> **Pip:** Run into them to collect them. Jump with the JUMP button. Grab all six on the dock!

*After:*

> **BOLT:** Six crystals. I counted them. They are very cold.

**Mission 2: First Dive** (Dive, level 1)

> **Pip:** Now the sea. Walk in and you swim. Hold JUMP to go up, DIVE to go down, and the stick swims you along.

*After:*

> **Rory:** It's so quiet down there.
> **TORPEDO:** Quiet? You should hear the whales. They never stop.

**Mission 3: TORPEDO Time** (TORPEDO through the rings, level 1)

*After:*

> **TORPEDO:** Every ring! You're a natural. I'm a natural too.

**Mission 4: Practice Drips** (Drip round-up, level 1)

*After:*

**Mission 5: Lock School** (Sluice gates, level 1)

*After:*

> **Admiral Frost:** Training complete. Cornwall, Agent Rory. The tide is turning, and not the right way.

*Leaving:*
