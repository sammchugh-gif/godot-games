## Act 1: The Sea Is Going Down

Harbours are drying up all over the world. Somebody is stealing the sea.

### POLARIS Sub Pen, Scotland

> **Admiral Frost:** Agent Rory. The sea is going down.
> **Rory:** Going down where?
> **Admiral Frost:** That is the question. Harbours are drying out from Cornwall to Hong Kong. Boats are sitting on the mud.
> **Pip:** So this mission is under the water. Rory, meet your new partner!
> **TORPEDO:** Hello! I am TORPEDO. I am a submarine. I am yellow. I am very fast.
> **BOLT:** I am BOLT. I am also very fast. On land.
> **TORPEDO:** Land is just the bottom of the sea with the water missing.
> **Pip:** You two will get on brilliantly. Training first. Follow the beams of light!

*After:*

> **Rory:** They're cold. And they sort of slosh.
