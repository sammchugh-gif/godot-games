# Agent Rory: Meltdown — the story bible

A first-person spy movie for the iPad's browser, and a completely new game:
new story, new agency, new cast, new countries and 24 new mini-games. It runs
on the engine built for *Agent Rory: Operation Eclipse* (saves, stars, voices,
the dossier, the map), with better graphics and a new chase engine on top.

The ideas are Rory's: make it like a spy movie, the bad guys are trying to melt
Antarctica and we have to stop them, ninety missions, new kids and new designs,
new countries, car chases, a snowmobile, better graphics, better games.

## The one-paragraph version

Baron Magnus Kaldera owns Kaldera Heating ("Keeping the world toasty since
1896") and lives inside a volcano. He has built six **Inferno Engines**: giant
drills that bore down through the Antarctic ice and pour volcanic heat in
underneath it. Fire all six and the ice sheet melts from below in a week, the
seas rise sixty metres, and every coastal city on Earth floods, unless the
world pays him a ransom of one trillion dollars. POLARIS, the secret agency
that watches the frozen ends of the Earth from a submarine called the
*Narwhal*, sends its youngest agent: Rory.

## The tone

A spy movie for a seven-year-old. Big stunts, gadgets, a villain with a
volcano, a henchman who is secretly soft, and a chase at the end of every
city. Nobody is ever hurt: villains get caught, fall in snowdrifts, get wet.
Every line is short enough to read aloud and funny where it can be.

The movie grammar is the point:

- **A cold open.** The game starts in the middle of the action in Greenland,
  before any briefing. Only when Greenland is done do the titles roll and
  Admiral Frost explain what is going on.
- **Establishing shots.** Arriving in a city, the camera sweeps round it
  before handing over to Rory, with the city's name across the screen.
- **Letterbox.** Cutscene dialogue plays with black bars top and bottom.
- **A set-piece in every city.** Each city's last mission is a chase or a
  stunt: eighteen of them across fourteen vehicles.
- **Gadgets.** Pip builds them, Rory uses them, and the chases have a BOOST
  and a TAG button.

## The agency: POLARIS

POLARIS guards the Arctic and the Antarctic. Its headquarters is the
*Narwhal*, a submarine that can surface through ice anywhere on Earth.
Briefings happen on its bridge, the world map is its navigation table, and it
carries Rory between countries (the flights on the map are the *Narwhal*
racing under the sea, or the POLARIS jet when it is faster).

## The routes

Three acts, six places each, five missions in each place: ninety missions.
The last mission in every place is a chase or a stunt.

**Act One: Thin Ice.** Something is melting Greenland's glaciers from
underneath in midwinter. The trail runs from a drilling rig on the ice to the
Norwegian shipyard that built it, to Baron Kaldera's gala in Monaco, where
Rory hears the words *Project Meltdown*, to Barcelona, where Scorch steals the
engine blueprints, to a test drill inside Santorini's volcano, and to a train
through the Canadian Rockies where Dr Cinder is caught and tells POLARIS about
the six Inferno Engines and the ransom.

**Act Two: Heatwave.** The engines are being built in pieces all over the
world. Dubai holds the money, the Atacama desert the guidance systems, Java's
volcanoes the magma cores, Seoul the control chips, the Himalaya a test
glacier, and Cape Town the ships that will carry the finished engines south.
Bruno is seen feeding penguins.

**Act Three: Meltdown.** South, fast. New Zealand's volcanoes are Kaldera's
last magma source, Ushuaia the last port before the ice. Then Antarctica: the
Peninsula, where Bruno changes sides, the Ross Ice Shelf where the engines are
drilling, the Ice Caves where Kaldera's submarine is docked, and finally Mount
Erebus, the real volcano at the bottom of the world, where Kaldera has built
his lair.

the brain and twelve for the thumbs. Twelve arrive in Act One and twelve more
in Act Two, each played twice in its own act as it gets harder, and all
twenty-four return in Act Three at full difficulty for the finale.

dart when close, or **escape**, staying ahead of whatever is behind you until
the finish. Hitting things costs ground. There is no way to lose, only to
take longer.
