Shardswarm: privacy policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

Shardswarm is a single-player game. We wrote it so that it does not need to know anything about you, and it doesn't.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking and no accounts of its own. It makes no network requests to us or to anyone acting for us.

What stays on your device

Your progress (records, unlocked ships, banked gems, awards, settings and a run in progress) is saved on your device so you can carry on where you left off. It never leaves the device through us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game Center and Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play Games (Android), the game sends your scores and awards to that service so they appear on leaderboards and in your achievements. Apple or Google handles that information under their own privacy policies. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent.

Purchases

The free download includes the first two sectors. The full game is a single one-time purchase handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your name, account or payment details. The store only tells the game whether the purchase is owned.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/sammchugh-gif/godot-games.
