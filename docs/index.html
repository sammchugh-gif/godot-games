Six games and a Sega Genesis. Tap one to play in the browser.

PLAY
Sonic Spin
A 3D Sonic act built on speed: loop, corkscrew, rails over the bay, homing chains, a torch-lit ruin, a wall run and a waterfall jump to the beach sprint. Keyboard, gamepad or touch.
PLAY
Paws of Fury 64
A 3D dog platformer. Ten open worlds, all of them wrong. Three dogs, triple jumps, ground pounds, and seven bones to sniff out in every world.
PLAY
Lizard Rocket
Swipe arrows onto the floor to steer the lizards into the rocket, past the snakes and around the holes. Twelve puzzles and a 90-second Stampede.
PLAY
Velocity Zero
Anti-gravity racing. Eight ships, three circuits, boost pads, rockets, missiles, mines and shields. Hold the iPad sideways.
PLAY
Blockfort: Boss Siege
Build a fort against the clock, then survive the golem that comes to tear it down. Ten rounds.
PLAY
Star Digger: Deep Boss
Dig down, collect every star, break the seal. Your stars are your lives against the boss below.
PLAY
Sega Genesis Arcade
A real Mega Drive emulator in the browser, with eight free homebrew games on the shelf: platformers, a trap-filled tower puzzle, an egg-catching chicken and a four-player star race. Add cartridges you own to play them on this device. On-screen pad on the iPad.

On an iPad: open a game, then Share → Add to Home Screen for a fullscreen icon. Touch controls turn themselves on automatically. In Sonic Spin, the left thumb is a floating stick and the right thumb has JUMP (tap again in the air to home in), SPIN, BOOST and DRIFT. In Paws of Fury, the left thumb is the stick, the right thumb has JUMP, BITE and POUND, and dragging the middle of the screen swings the camera. In Lizard Rocket, swipe across a tile to lay an arrow and tap an arrow to pick it up. In Velocity Zero, hold the phone or iPad sideways, drag on the left half of the screen to steer and use the AIRBRAKE and FIRE buttons on the right; in the voxel games, drag the MOVE pad to walk and drag elsewhere to look. In the Genesis Arcade, tap a game and then its Start button; the D-pad and A B C buttons appear on screen, and the bar under the game has fullscreen and save states.
