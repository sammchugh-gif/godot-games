Godot Games

Four games. Tap one to play in the browser.

Lizard Rocket
ChuChu Rocket with lizards. Swipe arrows onto the floor to steer the lizards into the rocket, past the snakes and around the holes. Twelve puzzles and a 90-second Stampede mode. Built for the iPad.
Velocity Zero
Anti-gravity racing in the spirit of the original WipEout. Eight ships, three circuits, boost pads, rockets, missiles, mines and shields. Built for the iPad with touch controls.
Blockfort: Boss Siege
Build a fort against the clock, then survive the golem that comes to tear it down. Ten rounds.
Star Digger: Deep Boss
Dig down, collect every star, break the seal. Your stars are your lives against the boss below.

On an iPad: open a game, then Share → Add to Home Screen for a fullscreen icon. Touch controls turn themselves on automatically. In Lizard Rocket, swipe across a tile to lay an arrow and tap an arrow to pick it up. In Velocity Zero, hold the phone or iPad sideways, drag on the left half of the screen to steer and use the AIRBRAKE and FIRE buttons on the right; in the voxel games, drag the MOVE pad to walk and drag elsewhere to look.
